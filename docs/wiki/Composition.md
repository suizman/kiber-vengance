## Clips and notes

Each of the five tracks keeps at least one clip. Clips support 1, 2, 4 or 8 bars and a 1/64–1/4 grid. Creating, duplicating, renaming, varying and deleting clips integrates with Undo/Redo. Deleting a used clip identifies its sections and reassigns references to another clip after confirmation.

The piano roll offers **Draw, Select and Navigate**, zoom, scrolling, marquee and multi-selection. Drag a note to move it and its edge to resize it; edit length and velocity. Duplicate, quantize and delete act on the selection. Duplication explains when the clip needs more space.

Copy/paste notes, move with arrows and resize with Alt + horizontal arrows. Free recording retains microtiming and sub-step lengths; quantized recording follows the grid. Velocity and MIDI expression are retained.

## Generate and transform

Key and scale guide chords, riffs, bass lines, arpeggios and beats. Mutation produces controlled variations. Note transformations include retrograde, inversion, double/half duration and deterministic humanization.

**Backing** creates two to six sections of bass, pad and drums in cinematic, driving or dark styles, preserving guitar and lead. Edit their clips, repeats and order in Arrange. **Auto sequence** organizes sections from existing clips.

## Clip character

Draw cutoff, volume or pan automation below the piano roll. Each clip retains its own instrument, arp, LFO, effects and macros. Duplicating copies its settings; creating starts from the active character. Faders and Solo/Mute belong to the track.

**▶ CLIP** alternates with **Ⅱ PAUSE** and plays active clips together. Solo hears one track. **Load to Compose** activates all five clips assigned to an arrangement section and opens the editor.

[Local AI](Local-AI.md) generates new editable clips from instructions/context without replacing originals.
