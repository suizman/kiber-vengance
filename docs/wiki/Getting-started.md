# Getting started

## Open and play

1. Open [the studio](https://studio.suizman.org). The companion is optional.
2. Press Play or a piano key: the browser needs a gesture to activate audio.
3. In **Compose**, choose lead, guitar, bass, pad or drums. `1`–`5` selects the five tracks.
4. Draw notes, play the keyboard or use generators. Arm recording to keep what you play.
5. Open **Sound** to change the clip's sound and audition with **▶ CLIP**. Solo isolates a track.
6. In **Arrange**, combine clips into sections. Add recorded audio in **Audio & sampler**.
7. Set levels in **Mix** and save a **Project Package** to include the audio in your backup.

## Four workspaces

| Workspace | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Compose | Clips, piano roll and composition tools; shared Sound inspector |
| Arrange | Song sections and the Audio & sampler tab |
| Live | Performance deck and Clip launcher |
| Mix | Channels, groups, meters and output |

Switching workspaces does not itself change the transport's clip/song mode. The Sound inspector is available throughout the studio.

## Transport

**50–220 BPM**, **4/4, 3/4 and 6/8**, swing, metronome and one/two-bar count-in. **Transport settings** contains controls that do not fit in the compact header.

Play/Stop uses the configured locator; **Pause** retains the cursor and resumes there, including within audio regions. Clips and songs have separate locators. The Arrange timeline jumps to an exact position.

## Before closing

The project autosaves in this browser. Export **Project Package** to move it with samples and takes; JSON stores structure and references only. [Saving and recovery](Projects-and-export.md).
