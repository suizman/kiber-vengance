# Scores and acoustic instruments

[English](Scores-and-instruments.md) | [Español](ES-Scores-and-instruments.md)
In **Compose → Import score**, choose MusicXML/MXL, MIDI, Guitar Pro, ABC, PDF or a PNG/JPEG image. Use MusicXML export from MuseScore, Sibelius, Finale or Dorico.

Review every part before inserting: choose its sound, split/merge staves, change notes and tempo/meter, listen, or view eight bars of notation. **Insert all reviewed sections** adds independent tracks and sections in one undoable edit. Matching parts on later pages reuse a track. **Add instrument / Edit track** controls names, order and extra tracks.

PDF/image recognition runs locally after an optional 150.2 MiB model download. Work page by page; crop/rotate difficult pages and correct errors. Printed notation works best. Handwriting, complex layouts, repeats and automatic instrument grouping may be wrong. Unknown instruments use piano. A cancelled page can be retried; completed download chunks and reviews are saved. The device must have enough memory; physical-phone reliability is not established by browser emulation.

**Resume saved review** resumes work. **Project sources** reopens original imports. Export corrected MusicXML per section; save **Project Package** to carry the originals/reviews with your song. JSON alone does not embed them. Generated singing remains desktop-only; score import and acoustic playback work on mobile too.
